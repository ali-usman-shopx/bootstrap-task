Bitters start the digestive process strong, while the Probiotic supports a thriving gut environment. Together, they conquer occasional bloating, improve regularity, and support the gut bacteria that keep your body feeling its best.

DIRECTIONS:

Bitters: Take two capsules of Bitters with your largest meals to prepare your digestive system for food.

Probiotic: Ages 5+: 1 capsule per day with your largest meal or as directed by your physician. For kids 8 and under, capsules can be opened and mixed with food or drink. Children under 3, please consult your healthcare practitioner.
